Privacy Policy

Brushdrop · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary: Brushdrop does not require an account, does not sell your data, and stores your brushes, palettes, and drawings locally on your device. We collect only anonymous usage analytics to improve the app.

1. Information We Collect

Brushdrop is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information inside the app.

  • User Content: Custom brushes, color palettes, and test drawings you create are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or access your artwork or files.
  • Anonymous Usage Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used (for example, which tabs, filters, or export actions are tapped) so we can improve the app. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.
  • App Configuration: We use Firebase Remote Config to deliver non-personal app configuration updates.

2. In-App Purchases

If you purchase Brushdrop Pro or other in-app items, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID details.

3. Third-Party Services

Brushdrop relies on the following third-party services, which may process technical device information (such as app version, OS version, and anonymized app instance identifiers) in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Config)
  • Apple App Store & StoreKit

4. Data Retention & Control

Because your brushes, palettes, and drawings are stored locally on your device, you can delete them at any time within the app or by uninstalling Brushdrop. Anonymous analytics data is retained according to Google Firebase's standard retention settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Brushdrop does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@brushdrop.app.
