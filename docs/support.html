Restoring Purchases

If you previously purchased BrushDrop Pro and reinstalled the app or switched devices, open BrushDrop and tap Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Exporting Brushes & Palettes

Brushes, brush sets, and color palettes can be exported directly from BrushDrop using the share/export actions and opened in compatible apps such as Procreate.
